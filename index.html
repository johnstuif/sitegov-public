How it works

From chaos to clarity in four steps.

Paste a list of domains or upload a spreadsheet. SiteGov creates a client record for each one.

2

Run an infrastructure scan

SiteGov scans DNS, SSL, WHOIS, hosting, email records, and provider signals. Live, from the source. No agents or integrations required.

Platform

Built for the work agencies actually do.

Every feature is designed for the specific realities of managing client web infrastructure at scale.

Infrastructure Snapshot

A complete view of every provider behind a client domain. Registrar, DNS, hosting, email platform, and SSL authority in one structured record.

Infrastructure Map

See how a client's infrastructure layers connect. Domain governance and service infrastructure organized into a clear, structured control map.

Provider Library

SiteGov recognizes hundreds of infrastructure providers across DNS, hosting, email, CDN, and registrar categories. Unknown providers are flagged for review.

Governance Records

Track the primary admin, backup admin, MFA status, credential location, and recovery documentation for each infrastructure asset.

Domain Scans

Automated infrastructure scans pull live data from DNS, RDAP, SSL, and HTTP headers. Trigger a scan at any time or let SiteGov run them automatically.

Findings and Risk Signals

Infrastructure and governance findings ranked by severity. High-risk signals surface immediately so your team can act before they become client problems.

Renewal Readiness

Track domain and SSL expiry across your entire client portfolio. Know what is renewing, when it expires, and who is responsible.

Client Portfolio Dashboard

A single view of every client, every domain, and every risk signal across your agency's portfolio. Sorted by severity. Always current.

DACS Scoring

The Digital Asset Control Score (0 to 100) measures infrastructure and governance health per domain. One number that reflects how controlled each client's web presence actually is.
